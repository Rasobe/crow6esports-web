Crow 6 eSports' run at the RLCS Club Kick-Off Open EU came to an end on Saturday 14 November. Diaxz, Gato and K94 lost 2-1 to Kami-1 in the first round of the day 2 losers bracket, in a series that went all the way to the deciding game.

## Down to the final game

Crow 6 started day 2 in the Pool B2 losers bracket, with no room for error and a best-of-three to win. Their opponents, Kami-1, were seeded 159th and, like Crow 6, had come through Friday with a 3-1 record.

The series went the distance, and Kami-1 took the third game to win 2-1 and move on. A narrow defeat against a higher-seeded side ends Crow 6's tournament, but they competed right to the last game.

## The tournament in numbers

Crow 6 entered the Open seeded 274th of 1,059 teams and leave with these numbers:

* **Series:** 3 won, 2 lost.
* **Games:** 7 won, 4 lost.
* **Wins:** 2-0 vs K-Team, 2-0 vs no u and 2-0 vs 1FA eSports.
* **Defeats:** 0-2 vs Scream / Kérian (seed 18) and 1-2 vs Kami-1 (seed 159).

Getting through day 1 in a field of more than a thousand teams, and beating higher-seeded opponents such as no u, is a solid starting point for Crow 6 in their first Open of the new Club Championship.

## What the team takes away

The Open brings valuable experience of a demanding format: best-of-three series, long days and matches with no second chance. It also gives Head Coach Rasobe and Extaahh plenty to work on, as the team returns to its weekly routine of macro and micro sessions, scrims and replay reviews, now with tournament footage to analyse.

## What's next

The European spot at the Club Kick-Off in London is still up for grabs. For teams that miss out, the road to the 2027 Club League runs through the Club Opens, with dates yet to be announced. We'll share news of Crow 6's next competition here on the site.

Thanks to everyone who followed the team over the weekend.
