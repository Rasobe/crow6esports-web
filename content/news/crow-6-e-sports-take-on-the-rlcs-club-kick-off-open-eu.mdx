Crow 6 eSports open their RLCS Club Kick-Off Open EU campaign on Friday 13 November at 6pm CET, in the open qualifier that starts the 2027 Rocket League season. Diaxz, Gato and K94 face Noxera eSport in round one, with just one European spot at the Club Kick-Off in London up for grabs.

## A new season, a new format

The RLCS gets a fresh structure in 2027: the Club Championship, split into three acts – Club Kick-Off, Club League and Club Finale. The Club Kick-Off Open is the way in for teams outside the invited group.

Only four regions get an Open: Europe, North America, South America and MENA, the strongest performers of the 2026 season. It's a one-off, LCQ-style qualifier (Last Chance Qualifier), so there's no second shot later in the year.

## Schedule and format in Europe

The European Open is played online on EU servers, with 1,059 teams signed up. Every match is 3v3, Crow 6's main competitive mode.

* **Friday 13, 6pm CET:** day 1, double elimination, best of three.
* **Saturday 14, 6pm CET:** day 2, double elimination.
* **Sunday 15, 6pm CET:** day 3, Swiss stage.
* **Friday 20, 5pm CET:** day 4, GSL group stage.
* **Saturday 21 and Sunday 22, 5pm CET:** days 5 and 6, hybrid elimination.

## What's at stake

The European winner earns three things:

* An official partnered spot in the Club Championship.
* A place at the Club Kick-Off in London, from 9 to 13 December at the Copper Box Arena, alongside 20 invited teams – 24 in total.
* A guaranteed spot in the Club League, which starts in January 2027 with 12 European teams.

Teams that miss out can still fight for a Club League place through the Club Opens, with dates yet to be announced.

## Crow 6's road through the bracket

Crow 6 go in with their starting three: Diaxz, Gato and K94. Head Coach Rasobe and Extaahh lead the preparation through the team's weekly routine of macro and micro sessions, scrims and replay reviews.

* **Seed:** 274 of 1,059 teams.
* **Pool:** A18, winners bracket round one.
* **First opponent:** K-Team.
* **Time:** Friday 13 November, 6pm CET.
* **Bracket:** [official start.gg bracket](https://www.start.gg/tournament/club-championship-europe-club-kick-off-open/event/3v3-bracket-europe-kick-off-open/brackets/2423096/3528239)

Seeding is based on each player's ranked MMR when registration closed. From there, it all comes down to what happens on the pitch. For a club founded in Huesca in 2020, playing the official RLCS qualifier is a chance to test themselves against Europe's open circuit.
