Crow 6 eSports are still in the hunt at the RLCS Club Kick-Off Open EU. Diaxz, Gato and K94 won three of their four series on Friday 13 November to book a place on day 2 of the European qualifier, which sends just one team to the Club Kick-Off in London.

## Day 1 at a glance

Seeded 274th of 1,059 teams, Crow 6 played four best-of-three series in the double-elimination stage:

* **Winners round 1:** Crow 6 2-0 K-Team.
* **Winners round 2:** Crow 6 2-0 no u.
* **Winners quarter-final:** Scream / Kérian 2-0 Crow 6.
* **Losers round 4:** Crow 6 2-0 1FA eSports.

That's six games won and two lost. The win over no u stands out, as they came in with a higher seed (239) than Crow 6.

## Growing into the day

It wasn't a perfect start. The staff felt the team began below their usual level, but they improved with every series: sharper rotations, more confidence on the ball and fewer mistakes at the back.

Their only defeat came against Scream / Kérian, seeded 18th and one of the strongest sides in the bracket. Crow 6 bounced straight back in the losers bracket with their best performance of the day, a clean 2-0 over 1FA eSports to seal qualification.

For a team that started the day well down the seeding list, finishing with three wins from four is a solid return, and a sign the work done in scrims and replay reviews is starting to show.

## Up next: Kami-1 on day 2

Day 2 is played on Saturday 14 November from 6pm CET, again in double elimination.

* **Opponent:** Kami-1, who also arrive with a 3-1 record.
* **Stage:** losers round 1, Pool B2.
* **Time:** 18:00 CET
* **Bracket:** [official start.gg bracket](https://www.start.gg/tournament/club-championship-europe-club-kick-off-open/event/3v3-bracket-europe-kick-off-open/brackets/2423097/3489474)

Starting in the losers bracket leaves no room for error: every series on Saturday counts on the road to Sunday's Swiss stage.

## What's at stake

The European Open winner earns a partnered spot in the Club Championship, a place at the Club Kick-Off in London (9–13 December) and a guaranteed spot in the 2027 Club League. The full format is in our [tournament preview](/en/news/crow-6-e-sports-take-on-the-rlcs-club-kick-off-open-eu).
