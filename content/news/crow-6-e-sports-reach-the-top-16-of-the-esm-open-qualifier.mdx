Crow 6 eSports finished among the top sixteen of the first ESM Open Rocket League qualifier on Saturday 21 November. Pychake and Gato won two rounds before going out in the round of 16 against VKSailen and npcrl2, the duo that went on to reach the semi-finals.

## A 2v2 with 119 registered teams

The ESM Open is the Rocket League tournament of the Esports Series Madrid, the city's municipal esports leagues, run by Sinfrenos League alongside Have Fun Gamers and Madrid in Game. This first qualifier was played online on Saturday at 17:00 CET, 2v2 and single elimination, with every round best of three except the semi-finals and the final.

The bracket opened 128 slots: **119 teams registered and 74 confirmed** during the check-in window. Crow 6 came in as the 15 seed.

## How the run went

* **Round of 128:** no opponent, straight through.
* **Round of 64:** win against GRASIENTOS FC.
* **Round of 32:** win against Pichi el mejor aura.
* **Round of 16:** loss to VKSailen / npcrl2.

That last pairing was no ordinary draw: VKSailen and npcrl2 kept going all the way to the last four. Crow 6 closed the qualifier in ninth place, among the eight teams that fell one round short of the quarter-finals.

The title went to "dando miedo ( giving scary )", who beat "y usted en frankfurt" in the final and took the slot for the LAN final on Saturday 28 November at the Esports Center Madrid in Game.

## Two competitions in two weeks

The qualifier comes a week after the RLCS Club Kick-Off Open EU, where Diaxz, Gato and K94 closed their first Open of the new Club Championship in **193rd place out of 922 teams**, with three series won out of five.

The two formats have little in common: the Open is 3v3 on a European scale, the ESM a national 2v2 played over an afternoon. For the coaching staff of Rasobe (Head Coach) and Extaahh, that contrast is useful material: a 2v2 exposes each player's rotations and boost management without the cushion of a third team-mate.

## What comes next

The ESM Open still has slots to give. The **second online qualifier is played on Sunday 22 November at 17:00 CET** and a **LAN Last Chance** follows on 28 November, the same day as the finals. Rounds from the quarter-finals onwards are broadcast on the [Sinfrenos League Twitch channel](https://www.twitch.tv/sinfrenosleague).
