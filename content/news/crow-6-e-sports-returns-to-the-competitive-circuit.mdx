Crow 6 eSports is back in competition. After three years, the club is starting a new chapter with a new roster and a strong desire to return to the Rocket League circuit.

## Who we are

Crow 6 eSports is a competitive Rocket League organization founded in 2020 in Huesca, Spain. Since then, our goal has been to develop players and teams and to compete at the highest level we can.

## A new chapter

In September 2025 we presented the new Crow 6 roster. You can meet all the players on the team page.

* Competitions: Storm League, Wall Rush, RLCS.

## How to follow us

We share every match and all our news on Instagram, X, Twitch, TikTok, YouTube and Discord. And if you want to improve your game, take a look at our coaching service.
